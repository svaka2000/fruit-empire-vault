# Sky Rivals - Weapons and Artillery

## Summary

- "Sky Rivals" is **SkyRivals ⚔️🐐** by Borshch games (place 16735970772). It is a sky-island PvP game. Up to 12 players fight until one is left. It has about 69 million visits [1][2]. It is not **RIVALS** by Nosniy Games, which is a separate first-person shooter. Search engines often mix the two up [3][4].
- Weapons do **not** carry over between matches. Everyone spawns with a Wooden Knife and 4 blocks. You find everything else during the match, in crates, in broken blocks, or on dead enemies [10][11][20][23].
- Robux pays for looks and options, not for found weapons. It buys weapon skins (from "Weapon Boxes"), classes (bought or rented), maps, and VIP [6][8].
- The weapon list has about 15 melee weapons, about a dozen guns and bows, 6 throwables, and some potions and special items. A **Nuke**, an **Explosive Staff**, and a **Caramba Gun** exist, but only admins can spawn them [23][32][33].
- Explosives are small and single-use. The **Fireball** deals about 50 HP on a direct hit and destroys blocks within 2 blocks of where it lands. Explosions set blocks on fire. Rain puts fire out faster [8][13].
- Structures break in a physical way. Blocks that players place have gravity. If you break the block holding up a bridge, the whole bridge falls [10]. **No boats are confirmed.** There are Airships and Factory maps with moving parts, but no source says those can be destroyed [8][29].
- Knockback (how far a hit pushes someone) is the main way to kill people, because falling into the void (the empty space under the islands) is instant death [14]. Bow knockback grows with how far you pull the string back. The Bat is labeled "high knockback" [8][26].

## How it works

### Which game this is

- A Roblox search for "sky rivals" returns SkyRivals ⚔️🐐 first, with 2,520 players online at the time of the search [3].
- Facts from the Roblox API: creator "Borshch games" (a verified group), max 12 players, avatars forced to R6 (Roblox's classic 6-part body), genre Action / Battlegrounds & Fighting, 69,036,699 visits, 39,692 favorites, last updated 2026-09-26 [2]. Rating is 71% (15.5K likes, 6.3K dislikes) [35].
- The description says "Released on 3/20/2026" [1]. A community wiki says 20 April 2026 [9]. The place itself was created on 2024-03-13 [2]. The patch notes mention an "Alpha currency migration", so there was probably an earlier alpha (test) period [8].
- Two community wikis exist. skyrivalsrblx.fandom.com says it is "Made by @borshch (owner) and @was_sim" [9]. Many stats on both wikis are editor guesses. The editors write things like "I haven't researched yet". Treat wiki numbers as rough.

### Match structure

- **Core mode:** last player standing, with up to 12 players [9]. The round timer was cut from 15 to 10 minutes in version 1.2 [8].
- **Spawn kit:** a Wooden Knife (14 damage, 0.6 s swing, low range, low knockback) and 4 blocks [10][20]. Pillars and Kit Wars give different starting gear [30].
- **Void:** on every map, falling into the void kills you instantly [14].
- **Modes:** Item Spawns (items drop straight into your inventory over time; only on the Pillars map), Kit Wars (pick a kit, no crates), Low Gravity, Team Wars, Team Kit Wars, Lava Flood, Quick Kill, Double Drops [14][15][16]. Kit Wars now offers 4 kit choices instead of 8 [8]. Nine kits were counted on 17 May 2026: Lumberjack, Hoplite, Knight, Sheriff, Ninja, Assassin, Vampire, Doctor, Pyromaniac [15].
- **Maps:** Pillars, Plates, Islands, Trees, Ranged Forest, Blossom, Cardboard Castle, Wall, Airships, Frozen Soil, Factory, Canyon, City, Beach [29], plus Lift (added in 1.4) [8]. A "B-Side" is a remixed version of a map. Pillars B-Side has an 11.1% chance to appear [14].
- **Scoring:** there are point bonuses named AIR KILL, AIR BATTLE, and Reckless. There are point multipliers named Zero-casualties and Untouchable [8]. Winning gives XP. The lobby has a weekly Highest Score leaderboard and a Highest Level leaderboard [8].
- **Classes (added in 1.4):** Kick, Marksman, Parkourist, Rifter, Thief, Juggler [8]. Known class rules: Marksman's guns pierce through enemies. Juggler's thrown potions trigger their effect. Rifter has 85 HP (raised from 80) [8]. Most other class abilities are not confirmed.

### How players get weapons

- **Crates** are boxes you open for loot [11]:
  - *Wooden Crate:* common melee weapons, basic ranged weapons, utility items.
  - *Red Crate:* "high-tier firearms, powerful explosives, and rare melees". Usually found in risky spots or the center island.
  - *Blocks Crate:* a stack of building blocks.
  - **Two ways to open:** hold E to fill a ring (fast and silent), or hit the crate until it breaks (speed depends on your weapon's damage) [11].
- **Breaking blocks:** planks, dirt, and stone give 1 block each. Logs give 4 [10].
- **Dead enemies** drop their items [23].
- **Trees:** breaking leaves drops Apples and Oranges [23].
- **Enchantments** are random bonus stats rolled on a weapon when it comes out of a crate. They apply to melee and ranged weapons, but not to the Wooden Knife or throwables [12]. One wiki gives these numbers:

| Enchantment | Chance | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Fierce | 35% | +10% damage |
| Nimble | 35% | +10% speed (equip, swing/fire, projectile) |
| Epic | 25% | +10% damage and +10% speed |
| Legendary | 5% | +20% damage and +20% speed |

  The other wiki says Fierce adds about 20% damage, and that Epic is rarer than Fierce or Nimble [25]. So these numbers are **not confirmed**.

- **Robux side (none of it is a found weapon)** [6][7]:
  - Weapon Box: 1 for 79 R$, 3 for 219, 10 for 699, 30 for 1,999. Lootboxes and weapon skins were added in the same patch, 1.5 [8]. The source does not say what is inside a box. It is probably skins.
  - Unlock a class: 399 to 799 R$. Rent a class: 19 R$ "for a limited time".
  - Buy a map or mode for the current server: 19 R$.
  - Place a bounty on a player: 20 R$.
  - Restore your streak: 49 to 499 R$.
  - VIP: 99, 169, or 289 R$ for 7, 15, or 30 days.
  - 2x XP game pass: 99 R$.
- **Gold** is the soft currency (earned in play). Liking the game and joining the group gives 100 Gold [1]. The shop refreshes every 12 hours [8]. The sources do not say exactly what Gold buys. Emote prices are mentioned [8].

### Explosives and how they break things

- **Block physics:** map blocks are anchored (fixed in place). Blocks that players place have gravity. "If the supporting base of a tower or bridge is destroyed, the entire structure will collapse" [10]. The wiki suggests breaking the bridge under an enemy to drop them into the void [10].
- **Pillars** are made of blocks that can't be broken [14].
- **Fireball** destroys blocks within a 2-block radius and sets things on fire [13].
- **Since 1.3.6, explosions set blocks on fire.** Fire clears faster in the rain (1.3.4) [8].
- **Axe** breaks wood instantly but not placed Blocks [24].
- **Breaking a teammate's blocks** was made harder in 1.3.3 [8].
- **Shield** blocks explosive attacks better since 1.3.3 [8].
- **Vehicles:** none confirmed. Airships has buttons [8]. Factory has a floating board that moves back and forth when you press two buttons. It also has crate spawners that make up to 15 crates in total, with no more than 3 out at once [29]. No source says whether airships or the board can be destroyed. **SkyRivals has no confirmed boats.**

### Building artillery projectiles in Roblox (added coverage)

SkyRivals' own code is not public, so this section covers how Roblox developers usually build lobbed shots like a mortar or cannon.
- **Simulate with raycasts, not physics parts.** FastCast is the common projectile library. Its creator, EtiTheSpirit, no longer maintains it. **FastCast2** (posted 24 Nov 2025) continues it. It describes itself as a library "designed to simulate thousands of projectiles without relying on physics replication", with raycast, blockcast and spherecast support and parallel scripting [41].
- **Server decides hits, clients draw.** A common setup: the server runs the shot for hit detection, and one remote tells every client to draw its own copy [42]. The known problem is that the drawn shot and the server shot drift apart when the shooter is moving. One fix is to send the fire position, direction and fire time, and have the server skip its copy ahead to match [42].
- **Show the arc before firing.** A Beam is a cubic Bézier curve, and a parabola can be drawn exactly with one. A 2021 DevForum resource computes `CurveSize0` and `CurveSize1` from the launch velocity, start point, gravity and flight time [43]. It builds on EgoMoose's projectile-motion tutorial [44]. The same math gives the landing point, which is where the warning circle goes.
- **Avatar note:** SkyRivals forces R6 avatars [2]. Fruit Empire forces R15 (`MorphToR15` in the Roblox API, read 2026-10-04) [45]. Weapon animations and hold poses cannot be copied across directly.

## What Fruit Empire should take

**Principles**

1. **Knockback is the PvP win condition near edges.** SkyRivals kills mostly through the void and through bridges collapsing [10][14]. Fruit Empire's sky arena duels can work the same way. Bats push people. Swords do damage.
2. **Charge-based knockback.** Copy the bow rule, "knockback depends on how hard you pull" [8], for the bat. Hold to charge. A longer charge gives more push. Rebirths raise the maximum charge tier.
3. **Explosives should damage health and break parts, not delete things.** The Fireball is about 50 HP of damage plus a small 2-block break radius [13]. Map this onto the plan where artillery drains fruit health. Each weapon has a splash radius, the damage falls off toward the edge, and fruit loses health over time instead of vanishing.
4. **Fire as damage over time.** Explosions ignite wood, and rain clears fire faster [8]. Wooden boats could burn after a hit. Fruit Empire's weather (if added) could counter it.
5. **Breakable boats from separate parts.** Use SkyRivals' rule that unsupported blocks fall [10]. Build boats from studded parts, each with its own health. When a part breaks, it comes loose. The boat sinks once enough hull parts are gone.
6. **Random stat rolls on weapons.** A small Fierce/Nimble/Epic/Legendary roll [12] adds variety without new art. If rolls are ever bought with Robux, or with currency bought with Robux, Roblox requires showing all odds as percentages that add up to 100%. Players in restricted regions must get a compliant alternative, checked with PolicyService `ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted` [37].
7. **Keep Robux on skins and options.** SkyRivals sells weapon skins in boxes and puts no found weapons behind Robux [6][8]. This fits the "weapon skins" item on the owner's wishlist.
8. **Clear warnings before damage.** The Tripmine beeps and flashes [28]. The Hunting Rifle's laser is visible to others [8]. Every big Fruit Empire explosive should show a warning circle or a sound before it hits.

## What to avoid

- **Hurting other players' fruit.** Stealing was removed on purpose. If artillery can drain another player's fruit on their plot, that brings back loss from other players in a new form. Limit damage to other players to the sky arena, duels, and boats at sea, unless the owner decides otherwise. Note that this is not hypothetical: the live TNT (2,500 shards) already deletes fruit on any plot, including other players', unless that plot is locked (local code) [46].
- **Unavoidable instant kills.** The Cursed Book (death after 40 s) and the Nuke fit a 10-minute round where everyone starts again [8]. They do not fit a persistent plot. Any Fruit Empire nuke should drain health and give a warning, not wipe a plot.
- **Self-knockback near water or edges.** The shotgun's kick reportedly pushes the user too [31]. That is fun in an arena. Near your own plot edge or the sea, it just feels unfair.
- **Copying wiki numbers.** Most SkyRivals stats are editor guesses ("i think", "haven't researched") [13][24][28]. Tune Fruit Empire's own numbers.
- **Selling power for Robux.** SkyRivals sells classes that change gameplay for 399 to 799 R$ [6]. Fruit Empire weapons already scale with rebirths. Selling top artillery tiers for Robux would undercut the rebirth path.
- **Many hidden or admin-only items.** Players keep asking about items they can never get ("wheres the vip only weapons?", "I NEVER KNOW THESE EXISTED") [31][33]. Keep the full weapon list visible in the Index or shop, with locks.

## Open questions

- What are the exact damage, splash radius, and cooldown numbers for any SkyRivals explosive? Only wiki estimates exist. In-game testing in a VIP server would answer this.
- Does SkyRivals' Grenade Launcher break blocks like the Fireball? Can the Airships or the Factory board be destroyed? Not confirmed.
- What is in a Weapon Box (skins only, or more)? What are the drop rates? Not confirmed. If the boxes are random and paid, the game must show odds [37].
- How many hotbar slots does SkyRivals have? What do Gold and the 12-hour shop sell? Not confirmed.
- What do the Hook and the Banana Peel do? What is "Villainbrine"? It is added and removed in almost every patch, and its spawn chance was cut in 1.5.1 [8].
- **For Fruit Empire:** what is fruit health actually for? Possible answers include PvP raids (which conflict with removing stealing), your own fruit, special target or boss fruit, or boats only. This choice decides who artillery can be aimed at.
- **For Fruit Empire:** do boats take damage only from artillery, or also from melee and fire?
