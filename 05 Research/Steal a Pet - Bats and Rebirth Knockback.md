# Steal a Pet: Bats, Slaps and Rebirth Knockback

Terms used in this note:
- **Bat / slap**: a melee tool. You click, and the player you hit gets pushed away.
- **Knockback**: how far a hit pushes a player.
- **Ragdoll**: the hit player goes limp and falls over for a moment and cannot control their character.
- **Rebirth**: you give up your cash and some of your units, and in return you get a permanent bonus (a money multiplier, more slots, new shop items).
- **Base lock**: a button that closes your base for a set number of seconds so nobody else can walk in.
- **Griefing**: hurting another player's game for no gain to yourself.
- **CCU**: concurrent users, meaning how many people are playing right now.

Which game is which. There are three different games with "Steal" and "Pet" or "Egg" in the name:
- **Steal a Pet** by BIG Games Pets (released June 2025). This is the main one in this note. [1][13]
- **Steal A Pet** by Dropout Interactive (created August 2026). It is a different game where you steal caged pets from animal bosses. [28]
- **Steal an Egg**. This is a separate game whose bats upgrade through the pet index. [30][31]

**Steal a Brainrot** by BRAZILIAN SPYDER is the original that made the slap ladder popular. [21][22]

## Summary

- In BIG's Steal a Pet, the **Bat itself never upgrades**. Everyone gets it free, and it has the weakest knockback in the game. The real upgrade ladder is **12 slaps** sold at the Merchant. Each slap needs one more rebirth (0 to 12), and each one "sends the target player back a bit farther" than the one before. [1][3][5][6]
- Steal a Brainrot works the same way. You get a free Bat with small knockback, and then a ladder of 12 slaps (Slap, then Iron through Splatter), one per rebirth from 0 to 11. [14][15]
- **Neither game publishes knockback numbers.** The wikis only say "a bit farther" or "stronger version". The slap cooldown is listed as "N/A". Exact numbers are **not confirmed**. [5][15]
- **What a hit does:** it makes a thief **drop the stolen unit**, which goes back to its owner's base. In Steal a Brainrot, a thief also cannot use any gear, walks about 41% slower (34 to 20) and jumps 40% lower. That makes thieves easy to slap. [1][15][18]
- **Anti-grief tools in Steal a Brainrot:** a 30-second base lock when you join, a 60-second lock button that gets +10 seconds per rebirth, a laser gate only you and your friends can walk through, and a late-game Grief Shield that blocks one hit. Steal a Pet uses the same lock idea (+10 seconds per rebirth) and also makes 17% of pets "Unstealable". [8][17][21][4]
- **Player complaints:** third players hitting a thief deleted the brainrot forever ("griefing"). The first fix was reverted after a day because of backlash. A later fix (January 31, 2026) makes the brainrot drop on the ground instead. Critics also called the game pay-to-win and full of cheat scripts. [19][22][23][24]
- **Knockback without stealing already works.** In **Slap Battles** there is no theft at all. Gloves show **Power** and **Speed** numbers (Default: 30 and 10, Diamond: 40 and 13). You spawn in a safe lobby and choose to enter island arenas, where the goal is to knock people off. There is also a 1v1 duel portal and a battle royale mode. [32][33][34][35][36][37]
- **The Index can unlock bats.** In Steal an Egg, finishing a biome page of the pet index unlocks that biome's bat look. There are 9 biome bats, from a starter wooden bat up to a katana. Fruit Empire already has an Index, so this pattern can be copied directly. [30]

## How it works

### Steal a Pet (BIG Games Pets): Bat and slap ladder

The **Bat** is given free when you join. It makes a thief let go of your pet, and it has the weakest knockback in the game. [1] It is listed at 1,000 coins in the items table, but you get it by joining. [2]

The slaps are sold at the **Merchant**, a coin shop. Every 2 Merchant items need 1 more rebirth, up to 12 rebirths. [3] Each slap page says the slap pushes the target "a bit farther" than the slap before it, from the Slap (farther than the Bat) up to the Error Slap (farther than the Magma Slap). [5][6]

- **Colors.** The names follow a material ladder: steel, gold, diamond, rainbow, dark matter, void, galaxy, then themed slaps. The exact colors of each model are **not confirmed**, because I only read the wiki text, not the images.
- **Non-slap tools on the same ladder.** Paint Grenade (rebirth 1) blinds players for a moment. Subspace Tripmine (rebirth 6) launches a player into the air and stuns them briefly. Red Hyperlaser (rebirth 9) stuns the target so they cannot move or steal for a short time. [2][11][12]
- **Robux knockback toys.** Double Slap costs R$199. Gravity Gun costs R$499 ("send players floating away"). Ban Hammer costs R$2,199 ("smack players to the moon"). [7] A free BIG Slap is given for liking the game and joining the group. [10]
- **Where bats can be used.** The wiki does not say whether there are no-hit zones. This is **not confirmed**. The only protection it lists is the base lock. [4][13]

### Steal a Pet: Rebirth

To rebirth you need specific pets kept in your base plus a coin amount. Rebirth wipes your pets and resets your coins. In return it gives a permanent coin multiplier, more lock time, +1 pet slot and new Merchant items. [4]

| 5 | 150m | 4x | 15 | 110s | Rainbow Slap |
| 8 | 10b | 5.5x | 18 | 140s | Galaxy Slap |
| 10 | 100b | 7x | 20 | 200s (+50s) | Godly Slap |

- The Rebirth 12 cost (1b) is lower than Rebirth 11 (500b). This is probably a **wiki typo**. Not confirmed.
- The wiki notes that Rebirth 10 says +10s in-game but really adds +50s. [4]
- A "Faster Rebirth" gamepass makes rebirths 25% cheaper for R$149. [9]
- Each pet has a 17% chance to spawn **Unstealable**. Huge pets are the exception. [8]

### Steal a Brainrot: Bat and slaps

The **Bat** (it used to be called "Tung Bat") is free. It knocks players down with small knockback. If the player you hit is holding a brainrot, the brainrot goes back to its spot in its owner's base. [15] The Bat and some starter-pack items stay in your inventory. All other bought gear disappears when you leave, so players use an auto-buy feature. [21]

The Slap costs $500, needs no rebirth, and reaches farther than the Bat. [15] The coin-shop ladder [14]:

| Rebirth | Slap | Cash |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Slap | $500 |
| 1 | Iron Slap | $2.5K |
| 2 | Gold Slap | $15K |
| 3 | Diamond Slap | $50K |
| 4 | Emerald Slap | $200K |
| 5 | Ruby Slap | $1M |
| 6 | Dark Matter Slap | $1.5M |
| 7 | Flame Slap | $2.5M |
| 8 | Nuclear Slap | $6M |
| 9 | Galaxy Slap | $15M |
| 10 | Glitched Slap | $25M |
| 11 | Splatter Slap | $75M |

- Each slap is described only as a "Stronger version" of the one before. [15] The Diamond Slap's text says "Charge up", but the wiki says it **cannot** actually be charged. [14]
- The later slaps add a side effect. Splatter covers the hit player's screen in paint. The Gummy Slap (rebirth 14) freezes the target in a gummy bear for 3 seconds. [15]
- **Cooldown:** the gear table lists "N/A" for every slap. [15] The event slaps show "0.75s" in a column labeled "Tooltip". That might be a cooldown, but it is **not confirmed**. [15]
- **Event slaps** come from event wheels at 0.5% or 0.25% odds. They add effects such as pinning a player to the ground, turning their controls backwards, making them invisible, or making them float and then dropping them. [14][15] The admin-only Dev Slap knocks players very far and leaves them on the ground for 5 seconds. [14]
- **Thief penalties:** a thief cannot use gear, and their walk speed drops from 34 to 20 and jump from 7.2 to 4.32. The owner gets an alert like "Someone is stealing your ...". [18]

### Steal a Brainrot: Base defense and rebirth

- **Lock:** your base is locked for 30 seconds when you join. After that, a lock button closes it for 60 seconds. Each rebirth adds +10 seconds, and VIP adds +10 seconds. [17][21]
- **Gate:** the base gate is made of red laser beams. Only the owner, and friends if allowed, can pass through. [21]
- **Height as defense:** Rebirth 2 adds a second floor and Rebirth 10 adds a third. The wiki says brainrots on higher floors are harder to steal. [16]
- **Traps** freeze players for 7 seconds, and you can own up to 5. [15]
- **Grief Shield** (rebirth 19, $10B, 20-second cooldown) blocks the next hit, so you do not ragdoll or drop the brainrot. [15][26]
- **Rebirth requirements:** cash plus specific brainrots. Rebirth 1 costs $500K plus Trippi Troppi and Gangster Footera. Rebirth 19 costs $30Qa plus La Grande Combinasion. [16]
- **Rebirth rewards:** a money multiplier (x0.5 at Rebirth 1 up to x19), a cash bonus, +10 seconds of lock time, +1 slot (every rebirth except the first), and new gear. Rebirth used to take every brainrot in your base. Now it takes only the required ones. [16]
- **Weapon price compared with rebirth cost:** at Rebirth 5 you pay $100M to rebirth, and the new Ruby Slap costs only $1M (1%). In Steal a Pet, Rebirth 5 costs 150m and the new Rainbow Slap costs 7.5m (5%). [3][4][14][16] So the real gate for weapons is the rebirth, not the price.

### Griefing and complaints

- **What griefing means here:** the owner leaves the server, and then a third player hits the thief. The brainrot had nowhere to go, so it was deleted forever ("voided"). The wiki says there are "no real consequences" for doing this. [19]
- One update made a hit brainrot drop on the ground for 2 minutes. It was removed a day later after "huge backlash". [19]
- The **Immigo update** (January 31, 2026) made hit brainrots drop as a physical item that anyone can grab, but only when the owner has already left. About 67% of players in a Discord poll voted for this. The downside is that it turned stealing into instant PvP fights over the dropped item. [24]
- Players also use slaps to fight over brainrots on the shared conveyor, before anyone owns them. One tip is to drink the Giant Potion, stand on the brainrot and slap people away while you buy it. [20]
- **Critics:** Polygon called the game pay-to-win ("the best items are only available for cash"). It also went viral through clips of children crying after their brainrots were stolen. [22] GameRant counted about 200 public cheat scripts on GitHub that automate cash collection, target locking and stealing. [23]
- TechWiser says the slaps "mostly do the same thing" and calls the high-tier slaps poor value. [25]
- **Live numbers on 2026-10-03:** BIG's Steal a Pet had 8 players online and was last updated 2026-02-06. [13] Steal a Brainrot had 98,739 players online. [27] (Why Steal a Pet faded: [inferring, not cited] it copied the Steal a Brainrot loop with nothing new.)

### Bats upgraded by the Index (Steal an Egg, Dropout's Steal A Pet)

- **Steal an Egg:** a bat hit ragdolls and flings the target, and they drop any egg they carry. Bear traps hold a player for 5 seconds and also make them drop their egg. Both tools work anywhere on the map. [31] Better bats come from a Rift Shop, from admin events, or from finishing a biome page of the pet index. [30]
- **Steal an Egg bat looks:** Forest is a starter wooden bat, Lake is a cyan glowing bat, Desert has a particle aura, Jungle is black with a red aura, Volcano is flame-themed, Abyss is dark and watery, Prehistoric is bone, Cosmic is space-themed, and Cherry Blossom is a katana. "Stronger bats send egg-carriers much farther." [30] No numbers are given.
- **Dropout's Steal A Pet:** a search-result snippet says the starter bat only reaches 4 studs, and that collecting 8 creatures in one biome's index lets you equip that biome's bat. The biomes listed are Lake, Desert, Jungle, Snow, Volcano, Abyss, Prehistoric, Space and Titan Temple. [29] **Not confirmed:** the article page itself would not load.

### Knockback without theft: Slap Battles

- Slap Battles is a fighting game about slapping people with different gloves. "The more slaps you have, the more gloves you can use." [38]
- Every glove shows a **Power** stat (knockback) and a **Speed** stat. Default has Power 30 (it used to be 20) and Speed 10. Diamond has Power 40 and Speed 13 and costs 15 slaps. [32][33]
- You **spawn in a Lobby**, choose a glove there, and walk through a portal to enter an arena. [34] The Normal Arena has 7 islands. Falling off is the main way to lose. [35]
- **Slap Royale** is a last-one-standing mode that needs 1,000 slaps to enter. [36]
- A **Duelist** glove ability pulls one opponent through a portal into a private 1v1 arena. [37]

### Roblox engine notes (for building it)

- `BasePart:ApplyImpulse(Vector3)` gives an instant push at the center of mass. Heavier assemblies need a bigger push. The docs give two cases. If the **server** owns the part, only a server script can call it, and a call from a client script does nothing. If a **client** owns the part through automatic ownership (a player's own character), it can be called from a client script **or** a server script. [39]
- **In practice, a standing character barely moves.** A Humanoid in the Running state pushes back to stay upright, so the same impulse that throws a loose part "basically" does nothing to a dummy. DevForum fixes: switch the Humanoid to the Physics (or Freefall) state for the push, or set a velocity instead of an impulse. [41]
- **Fruit Empire already solved this in the Crown arena.** On a hit, the server fires a "knockback" event so the victim's own client sets the velocity at once. The server also adds a `LinearVelocity` (MaxForce 1e6) to the victim's root for 0.12 s, so the push still lands if that client is slow. [42] Bats can reuse this same push function.
- `Touched` events follow physics ownership, so a hacked client can fake sword hits from across the map. The docs say to check every hit on the server. [40]

### What Fruit Empire already has (local code, 2026-10-04 export)

- **Arena knockback numbers** (Crown duels, no health): a tapped Strike pushes 40 studs/s sideways and 16 up. A Heavy pushes 74 and 26. The Royal Slam pushes 92 and 32. Every hit adds "stagger", and knockback is multiplied by (1 + stagger/100), up to 2.6x. [43] These are a ready-made unit for bat tiers (studs/s), so bat numbers can be compared with the sword.
- **A Lock button already exists.** It raises a dome for 60 s, then needs 30 s to recharge after it drops. Since stealing was removed on 3 Oct 2026, the dome does not keep people out. It only refuses TNT. [44]

## What Fruit Empire should take

These are suggestions built on the facts above. Stealing stays removed.

1. **One bat tier per rebirth, gated by rebirth, priced low.** Both games gate weapons by rebirth count and price them at only 1 to 5% of the rebirth they follow. [3][14][16] Fruit Empire can grant or sell Bat tier N at Rebirth N for a small cost, so the bat feels like a reward and not another wall.
2. **Color the bats by fruit rarity tier.** Both games use a material ladder (steel, gold, diamond, galaxy...). [3][14] Fruit Empire's ladder is already Common to Transcendent. Give each bat the color of the highest fruit tier the player has unlocked, so the bat shows a player's stage at a glance.
3. **Show real numbers on the bat card.** Neither steal game shows knockback numbers, and players complain that higher slaps feel the same. [5][15][25] Copy Slap Battles and print Power (knockback) and Speed (swing rate) on each bat. [32][33] Make every tier visibly push farther.
4. **Index bat skins.** Copy Steal an Egg: finishing a page of the Fruit Index unlocks a bat skin for that page. [30] It rewards collecting, it needs no stealing, and it fits the glossy retro look.
5. **Give bats a job without theft:**
   - **Knock players off sky islands, boats and the sea edge.** This is the Slap Battles island loop. [35] It works well with boats (breakable, 2 seats) and swimming fatigue. A player knocked into the water has to swim tired.
   - **A neutral event drop instead of stolen fruit.** Steal a Brainrot players already fight over unowned brainrots on the conveyor, and the Immigo update proved that "drop it when hit" is popular (67% of the vote). [20][24] Fruit Empire could spawn a rare fruit crate or a meteor in neutral ground. Whoever carries it home keeps it, and a bat hit makes the carrier drop it. Nobody loses fruit they already owned.
   - **An opt-in arena portal** (free-for-all or last-one-standing), like Slap Battles' lobby portals. [34][36] Pay out shards based on the player's stage.
   - **King's Throne:** bats push challengers off the crown platform. Sword duels stay a separate 1v1 portal, like Duelist. [37]
6. **Safe zones by default.** Make your own plot, the spawn and the shops places where strangers cannot hit you. Slap Battles' spawn lobby is a no-fighting area, and Steal a Brainrot's laser gate keeps strangers out. [21][34] Add a few seconds of protection when you join, like Steal a Brainrot's 30-second join lock. [21]
7. **A Grief Shield as a later reward.** It blocks one hit, then has a cooldown. Steal a Brainrot puts it at rebirth 19 with a 20-second cooldown. [15] Fruit Empire could give it out earlier, because bats here are for fun and not for defense.
8. **Rebirth bundle, same shape as both games:** multiplier, +1 storage, a new bat, and some extra cash so the restart is not slow. [4][16] Fruit Empire's planned hard requirement (full index + shards + money) is stricter than both games. They ask only for specific units plus cash.
9. **Keep bats permanent.** Steal a Brainrot makes players rebuy gear every session (auto-buy exists to fix that). [21] Fruit Empire bats should stay forever once unlocked.

## What to avoid

- **Hits that destroy owned progress.** Deleting brainrots caused the biggest backlash and the "kids crying" coverage. [19][22] In Fruit Empire a bat hit should never cost a player owned fruit, pets or storage.
- **Pay-to-win knockback.** Polygon's main criticism was that the best items cost real money. [22] Sell bat skins and effects for Robux, not more Power. (Ban Hammer at R$2,199 is the kind of item to skip. [7])
- **Trusting the client for hits.** Hacked clients can fake Touched events, and Steal a Brainrot has around 200 public scripts. [23][40] Check distance and cooldown on the server.
- **Tiers that only change color.** Higher slaps were called poor value because they "mostly do the same thing". [25]
- **Strangers hitting you inside your own base.** Steal a Brainrot needed traps, sentries and lock timers just to keep bases livable. [15][20]
- **Copying the loop with nothing new.** BIG's Steal a Pet sits at 8 players while the original has around 98k. [13][27] (The cause is [inferring, not cited].)

## Open questions

- What are the real knockback forces and slap cooldowns in Steal a Brainrot and Steal a Pet? The wikis give none. Is the "0.75s" event-slap value a cooldown? A screen recording measured in Studio would answer this.
- Is the Dropout Steal A Pet "8 creatures per biome unlocks a bat" mechanic real? [29] The page was blocked.
- Should Fruit Empire allow any PvP on plots, or only in neutral zones, arenas, islands and boats?
- Should bats also hit fruit? The artillery plan says explosives drain fruit health. Bats should probably push only players, so the two systems stay separate.
- How much Power should each tier add, and how big is the hit cooldown? This needs a play-test in Studio. Start from the arena's own numbers: Strike 40 studs/s with a 0.42 s cooldown, Slam 92 studs/s. [43] A Bat 0 below the Strike and a top bat near the Slam is one possible range (proposal, not tested).
