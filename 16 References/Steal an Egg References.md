# Steal an Egg references (9 Nov 2026)

Samarth sent these on 8-9 Nov 2026, between 23:56 and 00:04. They set the bar for Fruit Empire's GUI, pets, eggs and bats. All but one come from the Roblox game Steal an Egg. The one from our own game is marked "ours". Player names are blurred, because this vault is public. Back to [[Knowledge Base - Start Here]]. How we reach this level is in [[Reaching Steal an Egg's Level]].

## What he asked for

- GUI: "make our GUI fit our theme and even better than the reference GUI, it has to have a lot of dopamaine."
- Fonts and colours: "i dont like the claude fonts and colors, i would like you to use something better."
- New art: "either use figma, or make chatgpt prompts that will make the UI for you to wire in."
- Inventory: "i want bat or pickaxe to be in the acctual inventory as well."
- Pets: "more complex pets". The last two pet shots "arent pixelated like ours". "instead of redoing everything just make tweaks to ours to make it on this level."
- Pets that act: "make the pets super animated as well they need to walk around sit down talk emojis etc like you should have full control of them."
- Bats: "here is reference for bats. make it better than these".
- The pet list to study: https://www.eldorado.gg/blog/steal-an-egg/steal-an-egg-all-pets-index/

> [!note] A reference game is style only
> Use these for the look and the feel. Fruit Empire keeps its own fruit theme, its own items and its own rules (see [[Samarth's Taste]]).

## Panels

**Inventory.** "All Items" with "Pets: 146/250", side tabs Pets, Eggs and Gear (the Gear icon is a bat), a search box, pet cards with name and weight, and a hotbar of ten slots. The bat sits in slot 1 and a stack of three eggs in slot 2.
![[sae-inventory-all-items-hotbar.webp]]

**Pet Index.** Blue title bar with stripes, red X, a world picture on the left, cards in rarity colours (unseen ones are "???" silhouettes), a progress bar "6/8", a details box (name, rarity, money per second), a Rewards box with a CLAIMED! button, and "Unlocked: 64/126" along the bottom. Our Index already follows this layout.
![[sae-pet-index.webp]]

**Sell Pets and Sell Eggs.** Lime title bar with an icon, Sort By (Weight, Value), Select All, cards with weight, money per second in green and the sale price on a dark plate, and a Total Value footer with a Sell button. Eggs and Pets tabs sit outside the panel on the right.
![[sae-sell-pets.webp]]
![[sae-sell-eggs.webp]]

**Fuse Machine.** Purple-magenta title bar, a two-line headline ("Bring 3 same Pets to Fuse", then "Better pets give more Luck" in green italics with a clover), three slots that turn into pet cards with a Return button, pipes that light up as slots fill, a mystery silhouette in the middle, and a "3 Pet Left" button. A small "3 pets = 1 bigger pet" help card sits beside it.
![[sae-fuse-machine-empty.webp]]
![[sae-fuse-machine-one-pet.webp]]

**Growing Eggs.** Peach title bar with a yellow-orange Grow All button, one row per egg with its picture on a sunburst and a green Open button, and a red tab to slide the list away.
![[sae-growing-eggs-panel.webp]]

**Shop.** Green title bar, "-- FEATURED --" in yellow, a banner for the Crystal Egg with a rainbow "New!" tag, "Limited Time!", a live countdown, each pet's chance (39% down to a 0.5% "DIVINE!" in a rainbow frame), Robux buttons (1, 3, 10 and 50 eggs, the 50 with a struck-out old price), purple gift buttons, and side tabs Featured, Stars, Speed and Money, each a big illustrated icon with its word.
![[sae-shop-featured-crystal-egg.webp]]

## Pets and eggs

**"This is an example of a good quality pet."** A big guardian beast on a plot, "602Kg" with a female sign over it, money popups. It is built from angled, chunky shapes, not cubes, and every face still has studs. Blue armour plates with tan trim, tan horns, glowing cyan inlays (a crescent moon, a star), a yellow diamond gem, blue flowers, leaf bits floating off it, and a thin white outline around the whole pet.

**Its egg.** Rounded white and gold petals around a blue core, all studded, glowing and sparkling on the plot.

## Bats

**Bats come from the Index.** Finishing a page unlocks an "Equip Bat!" button. The best one is a golden spear with a halo ring.
![[sae-bat-index-equip-bat.webp]]
![[sae-bat-golden-spear-held.webp]]

**Every bat in the game** (a 20-second video, one frame per second below). In order: Noob Bat, Noob Forest, Lake Bat (see-through blue with bubbles), Desert Bat (gold with sand swirls), Jungle Bat (dark green with red vines and leaves), Snow Bat (glowing ice), Vulcano Bat (black with flames), Abyss Ocean Bat (white with foam), Pre Historic Bat (black bone with spikes), Cosmic Bat (purple rings spinning round it), Cherry Blossom Sword, Titan Temple Axe (gold with sparks), Angels & Demons Spear, Enchanted Forest Hammer (a wooden hammer in vines and flowers), then events: Racing Bat (chequered, with a torch), two glowing lantern-maces, a banjo, a Pumpkin Scythe (fire) and an Alien axe with a green orb. Each world has its own look and its own particles. Later "bats" are swords, axes, spears and hammers.
![[sae-all-bats-video-frames.png]]
