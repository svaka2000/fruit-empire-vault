# ChatGPT art prompts (9 Nov 2026)

Prompts Samarth runs in ChatGPT to make the new art. Claude cuts the sheets up, uploads the pieces to Roblox and wires them into `UIKit`. Paste the style message first, in one ChatGPT chat, then one sheet per message. Files to attach are in `~/Downloads/FE ChatGPT attach/`. Why: [[Reaching Steal an Egg's Level]]. Back to [[Knowledge Base - Start Here]].

> [!note] No words in the art
> Words are added in game with FredokaOne, so they stay sharp and can change. The only exception is sheet 11, a layout mockup to agree on.

## Style (send first)

```
I'm making UI and art for my Roblox game Fruit Empire (fruit gardens, a fruit castle, a sky world, a crystal mine, pets made of fruit). Use this style for every image I ask for in this chat: chunky, glossy, cartoon game art like the top Roblox simulators (Steal an Egg, Pet Simulator 99), but juicier. Saturated candy colours. Every shape has a thick dark outline (#1B1530). A bright highlight along the top edge and a darker shade at the bottom, so everything looks 3D and squishy. Faint Roblox stud bumps on flat areas. Clean, crisp edges. No text, letters or numbers anywhere unless I ask. Transparent background (PNG with alpha). Straight front view, no perspective, nothing cut off at the edges, lots of empty space between items. Reply "ready" and wait for my sheets.
```
